# Chapter 49 — SA:MP: San Andreas Multiplayer, the Client in the Tree

> **Goal of this chapter:** account for the one thing in the working tree that is *not* Rockstar's — the
> **SA:MP** (San Andreas Multiplayer) client: `samp.dll`, `samp.exe`, and the `SAMP/` asset folder. SA:MP is
> third-party and closed-source, so this chapter documents it the only honest way — **from its own shipped
> files**, the same discipline the rest of the encyclopedia applies to `gta_sa.exe`. Two findings frame it:
> SA:MP does **not replace the engine, it wraps it** — its added content ships in the *base game's own data
> formats* (IMG/IDE/IPL/COL), and `samp.dll` is a **client hook + RakNet network layer** (version 0.3.7-R5)
> that synchronises player state over UDP.

## 49.1 Why SA:MP belongs in the encyclopedia — and how it is handled

Two things make SA:MP worth a chapter. First, its files are *in the tree* — by the project's "comb every
file" standard they must be accounted for. Second, and more interestingly, SA:MP is a live demonstration of
the base engine's extensibility: everything the earlier chapters decoded about IMG, IDE, IPL and COL is
exactly what SA:MP *uses* to ship its extra objects. A multiplayer total-conversion did not need new file
formats — it needed model IDs above the base range ([C49.1](01-reuses-the-base-engine.md)) and a network
layer bolted onto the running process ([C49.2](02-the-network-client.md)).

The evidentiary handling is different from every other chapter and stated plainly: SA:MP is **closed-source
third-party software**. `gta-reversed` does not cover it, and this project does not disassemble a proprietary
client to method-level detail. So C49 documents SA:MP **structurally** — versions, formats, the network
taxonomy, the hook surface — all read from its own shipped strings and file headers, and marks the parts that
would require deep RE of a closed binary (the `.saa` archive, the exact sync struct fields) as ⏳ open. That
is the honest boundary: what the shipped files *state* is ✅; what would need reversing the client's logic is
left open.

## 49.2 The shape of SA:MP, in one paragraph

`samp.exe` launches `gta_sa.exe` and injects `samp.dll` into it ([C0](../C0-Binary-Identity/C0-Binary-Identity.md)'s
process). `samp.dll` then hooks **Direct3D 9** (to draw its own chat/HUD over the
[C40](../C40-Render-Pipeline/C40-Render-Pipeline.md) frame — the `D3DXCheckVersion` string), hooks **input**
(`GetAsyncKeyState`, over [C46](../C46-Input-Devices/C46-Input-Devices.md)), and opens a **RakNet** UDP
connection to a server. Each frame it packages the local player's state into a **sync packet** — on foot
(`ID_PLAYER_SYNC`), in a vehicle (`ID_VEHICLE_SYNC`), aiming (`ID_AIM_SYNC`), as a passenger, a trailer, an
unoccupied vehicle, or a spectator — and applies incoming packets to the other players' peds/vehicles, which
are ordinary [C4](../C4-Entities-And-Pools/C4-Entities-And-Pools.md)-pool entities driven by the network
instead of the [C41](../C41-Ped-AI-Tasks-Wanted/C41-Ped-AI-Tasks-Wanted.md) AI. Its extra models come from
`SAMP.img`/`SAMP.ide` in the base formats, its audio from **BASS**, and admins control servers through
**RCON**. It is, precisely, a wrapper: the retail engine still runs the world; SA:MP feeds it networked
inputs and draws an overlay.

## Key takeaways

- SA:MP is a **third-party client wrapper**, not a new engine: `samp.exe` injects `samp.dll` (v**0.3.7-R5**)
  into `gta_sa.exe`, hooks Direct3D 9 + input, and networks over **RakNet**.
- Its added content ships in the **base game's own formats** — `SAMP.img` (VER2 IMG, 1,673 entries),
  `SAMP.ide` (objects at 18631+), `SAMPCOL.img` (COL) — a live proof of the engine's extensibility that
  [C7](../C7-RenderWare-Stream/C7-RenderWare-Stream.md)/[C11](../C11-IDE-And-IPL/C11-IDE-And-IPL.md)/[C6](../C6-Collision/C6-Collision.md)
  document.
- Multiplayer is a **sync-packet taxonomy** (player/vehicle/aim/passenger/trailer/unoccupied/spectator) over
  UDP; documented structurally because the client is closed source (`.saa` internals + exact struct layouts
  are ⏳).
