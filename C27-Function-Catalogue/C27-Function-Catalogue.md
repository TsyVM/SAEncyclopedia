## Why this chapter, and why now

C0 established *which bytes* are being described and *where* 492 function bodies actually live. It
deliberately stopped there — identity is a precondition for naming, not a substitute for it. Until now
the 492 were addresses and byte counts: real, verified, and anonymous.

This chapter's method follows the discipline [X1](../X1-SDK-Cross-Reference/X1-SDK-Cross-Reference.md)
already set for this project: an independent, external RE effort is a **lead**, worth exactly as much
trust as its own track record earns, and it earns that trust by being checked against the file, not by
being cited. Where C0.3 found the *NiTools* automated dump templated and 99.2 % blind to `.HOODLUM`, this
chapter finds the opposite result from a different kind of source: `gta-reversed` is not an automated
scanner but a hand-written, actively-maintained source reconstruction, and every one of the twelve
addresses independently disassembled and read here **agrees with the semantics its recovered name
implies** — see [C27.3](03-verification-and-the-remaining-124.md) for the disassembly of each.

**Next:** with 75 classes now anchored to real addresses, the natural follow-on is a class catalogue —
turning `CGarages`, `CReplay`, `CEntryExitManager`, and the rest of the 75 into their own subsystem
entries, the way `CStreaming` already has one in C2.
