## 42.1 Why this is the bridge, not a new island

[C13](../C13-Vehicle-Data/C13-Vehicle-Data.md) stopped where the data file stopped: it proved `handling.cfg`
is 210 rows of text with a known column layout, but a text row is not a simulation. The obvious next question
— *what does the engine turn each row into?* — is exactly what this chapter answers. Each row is parsed
(`LoadHandlingData` @`0x5BD830`) into one `tHandlingData` struct of **224 bytes**, and the 210 of them live
in a contiguous array indexed by the vehicle's `handlingId`. The proof that it is 224 bytes is not a header
claim: the engine's own accessor `HasFrontWheelDrive` computes `handlingId × 0xE0` with an `imul` to reach a
struct, and the 210 count is corroborated three ways — the parallel `VehicleNames` array of 210 entries, the
manager's array declaration, and C13's independently-counted 210 rows. Data chapter and physics chapter now
meet at the same number, from opposite directions.

## 42.2 The two halves of vehicle physics

Vehicle physics is two structures working together. **`tHandlingData`** is the *parameters* — the tuning
knobs from `handling.cfg`: mass, drag, centre of mass, traction, brake force, suspension stiffness, steering
lock, drive type. **`CPhysical`** is the *state and integrator* — the live rigid body every vehicle inherits:
its position comes from `CEntity`, but its velocity (`m_vecMoveSpeed`), spin (`m_vecTurnSpeed`), mass and
moment of inertia (`m_fMass`, `m_fTurnMass`) live here, and its `ApplyForce`/`ApplyTurnForce` methods are the
generic Newtonian integrator. Each frame the vehicle reads its `tHandlingData`, computes forces (engine,
brakes, tyres, suspension), and hands them to its `CPhysical` base to move the body. The handling file is the
character of a car; `CPhysical` is the physics that character plays out. That `CPhysical` is *shared* with
[C25](../C25-Object-Physics/C25-Object-Physics.md)'s dynamic objects is why a crate and a car obey the same
core integrator — only their parameters differ.

- `handling.cfg`'s 210 rows ([C13](../C13-Vehicle-Data/C13-Vehicle-Data.md)) become

  car, `CPhysical` integrates it, and the same `CPhysical` underlies [C25](../C25-Object-Physics/C25-Object-Physics.md)'s objects.
- The 210 is a cross-subsystem anchor: the data chapter and the physics chapter reach the same count from
  opposite ends.
